========================================
CHAPTER 1: CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICIES
========================================

1.1 Subscription and Service Agreement
-----------------------------------------
All customers must accept the Terms of Service (ToS) and Acceptable Use
Policy (AUP) before accessing ACME services. The ToS is available at
https://acme-corp.example.com/legal/tos and is incorporated by reference
into all subscription agreements.

Account Types:
- Individual Account: Single user, non-transferable
- Organization Account: Multi-user with role-based access control
- Enterprise Organization Account: Unlimited users with SSO integration

Billing Definitions:
- Monthly Billing Cycle: Calendar month, prorated for mid-month signups
- Annual Billing: January 1 - December 31, invoiced quarterly
- Service Credit: Credits applied before taxes, non-transferable

1.2 Service Level Agreement (SLA)
------------------------------------
ACME guarantees the following uptime commitments per service tier:

Platinum (Enterprise Edition):
- Compute Availability: 99.99 percent (maximum 4.3 min downtime/month)
- Storage Durability: 99.999999999 percent (eleven nines)
- Network Availability: 99.99 percent
- Response Time (Critical): 1 hour (24/7)
- Response Time (Standard): 4 hours (business hours)

Gold (Professional Edition):
- Compute Availability: 99.95 percent (maximum 21.9 min downtime/month)
- Storage Durability: 99.9999999 percent (nine nines)
- Network Availability: 99.95 percent
- Response Time (Critical): 4 hours (business hours)
- Response Time (Standard): 24 hours (business hours)

Silver (Starter Edition):
- Compute Availability: 99.9 percent (maximum 43.8 min downtime/month)
- Storage Durability: 99.99999 percent (seven nines)
- Network Availability: 99.9 percent
- Response Time (All): 24 hours (business hours)

SLA Credits:
- For each 0.1 percent below guaranteed uptime, customer receives 10 percent
  monthly service credit
- Maximum monthly credit: 100 percent of monthly subscription fee
- Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident
- Credits do not include third-party service downtime
- Scheduled maintenance (max 4 hours/month, announced 72 hours in advance)
  does not count against SLA

1.3 Cancellation and Refund Policy
-------------------------------------
Cancellation:
- Monthly subscriptions: Cancel anytime, effective end of current month
- Annual subscriptions: Cancel anytime, effective end of current contract
  year. Early termination fee: 25 percent of remaining contract value.
- Enterprise contracts: 30-day written notice required
- Upon cancellation: Customer has 30-day grace period to export data
- After 30 days: All data permanently deleted, not recoverable

Refund Eligibility:
- Software subscriptions: Full refund within 30 days of initial purchase
  (no questions asked)
- Prorated refunds: Available for annual subscriptions cancelled after
  the 30-day window, calculated as: (remaining days / 365) * annual price
  minus a 5 percent early termination processing fee
- Hardware products: 14-day return window from delivery date
- Refund method: Original payment method only
- Processing time: 5-10 business days from approval

Special Cases:
- Defective hardware: Full refund plus free return shipping, 90-day window
- Service outage credit: Automatic for SLA violations, no request needed
- Promotional pricing: Refunds calculated at promotional rate used

CHAPTER 2: PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION
========================================

2.1 Data Collection and Use
-----------------------------
ACME collects and processes the following data categories:

1. Account Information: Name, email, company, billing address, tax ID
2. Usage Data: API calls, resource consumption, feature usage, logs
3. Payment Information: Stored with PCI DSS Level 1 compliant
   payment processor. ACME does not store full credit card numbers.
4. Support Data: Chat logs, email communications, support tickets

ACME does NOT:
- Sell customer data to third parties for marketing purposes
- Access customer data without explicit permission (except for legal/
  compliance requirements)
- Use customer data to train AI models without opt-in consent

2.2 Data Residency and Sovereignty
-------------------------------------
- US Customers: Data stored in US-East or US-West data centers
- EU Customers: Data stored in EU-Central (Frankfurt) or EU-West (Ireland)
- APAC Customers: Data stored in AP-Northeast (Tokyo), AP-Southeast
  (Singapore), or AP-Southeast-2 (Sydney)
- Cross-border transfers: Protected by EU Standard Contractual Clauses
  (SCCs) with additional encryption safeguards
- Data localization: Enterprise customers can specify exact data center

2.3 Data Subject Rights and GDPR Compliance
----------------------------------------------
ACME provides the following rights to all data subjects worldwide:

1. Right of Access: Request copy of all personal data held (free, within
   30 days)
2. Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
3. Right to Erasure: Delete all personal data (30-day completion
   window after verification)
4. Right to Portability: Export data in machine-readable format
   (CSV, JSON, Parquet)
5. Right to Restriction: Limit processing of personal data
6. Right to Object: Opt out of processing for legitimate interests

Request Process:
- Submit via self-service portal or email to privacy@acme-corp.example.com
- Identity verification required (government-issued ID or corporate email)
- Standard response time: 30 days (extendable to 60 days for complex
  requests)

========================================
CHAPTER 3: ACCEPTABLE USE AND SECURITY
========================================

3.1 Acceptable Use Policy
---------------------------
Prohibited activities include:
- Cryptocurrency mining without explicit authorization
- Distribution of malware, spam, or phishing campaigns
- Unauthorized access attempts to ACME infrastructure or other tenants
- Activities violating local, state, federal, or international laws
- Resale of ACME services without a partnership agreement

3.2 Encryption and Key Management
------------------------------------
ACME provides three encryption options:

1. ACME-Managed Keys (default):
   - AES-256 encryption at rest
   - TLS 1.3 in transit
   - Keys rotated every 90 days
   - ACME holds encryption keys

2. Customer-Managed Keys (CMK):
   - Available on Professional and Enterprise tiers
   - Customer manages keys via supported KMS (AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault,
     Google Cloud KMS)
   - ACME CANNOT access encrypted data with CMK enabled
   - Additional cost: $1.00 per 10,000 key operations

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK):
   - Available on Enterprise tier only
   - Customer provides own encryption keys via cryptographic protocol
   - Hardware Security Module (HSM) support for FIPS 140-2 Level 3
   - Zero-knowledge architecture: ACME has no access to keys or data
   - Additional cost: $500/month per key vault
