=== ACME CORPORATION POLICIES ===

Return and Refund Policy
------------------------
ACME Corporation offers a 30-day return policy for all software subscriptions
and a 14-day return policy for physical hardware products.

Software Subscription Returns:
- Full refund if cancelled within 30 days of purchase
- No questions asked refund policy
- Prorated refunds available for annual subscriptions cancelled after 30 days
- Refund processing time: 5-10 business days
- Refunds are issued to original payment method

Physical Hardware Returns:
- 14-day return window from delivery date
- Products must be in original packaging with all accessories
- Return shipping is free for defective items
- Customer pays return shipping for non-defective returns
- Restocking fee: 1% for opened items, 0% for sealed items

Electronics Return Policy:
- All electronic devices (servers, networking equipment, storage devices)
  carry a 14-day return period
- Electronics must be factory-reset before return
- Original receipt or order confirmation required
- Defective electronics can be exchanged within 90 days
- Extended warranty available for electronics: $199/year covers
  accidental damage, power surges, and component failure

Privacy Policy Summary:
- ACME collects minimal customer data required for service delivery
- Customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- Customers can request data deletion at any time
- ACME never sells customer data to third parties
- Data retention: Active accounts retain data, deleted accounts purge within
  30 days of deletion request
- GDPR and CCPA compliant

Service Level Agreement (SLA):
- Platinum Tier (Enterprise): 99.99% uptime, 1-hour response time
- Gold Tier (Professional): 99.95% uptime, 4-hour response time
- Silver Tier (Starter): 99.9% uptime, 24-hour response time
- SLA credits: 10% monthly credit for each 0.1% below guaranteed uptime
- Maximum monthly credit: 100% of monthly subscription fee
- Exclusions: Scheduled maintenance (max 4 hours/month), force majeure

Billing and Payment:
- All major credit cards accepted (Visa, MasterCard, Amex)
-ACH bank transfer available for annual plans
- Invoices generated on the 1st of each month
- Annual plans receive 15% discount vs monthly billing
- Enterprise customers can request Net 30 payment terms
- Late payment: 1.5% monthly interest after 15-day grace period
- Service suspension after 30 days of non-payment
- Data retained for 90 days after service suspension
