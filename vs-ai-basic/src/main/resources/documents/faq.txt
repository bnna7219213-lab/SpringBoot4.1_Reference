=== ACME CORPORATION - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===

Q: How do I upgrade my subscription from Starter to Professional?
A: You can upgrade your subscription at any time through the ACME Dashboard:
   - Log in to your account at https://dashboard.acme-corp.example.com
   - Navigate to Billing > Subscription Plans
   - Select "Professional Tier" and confirm
   - The price difference is prorated for the remaining billing period
   - New resources are available immediately after upgrade

Q: How long does it take to process a refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days from the approval date.
   For software subscriptions cancelled within 30 days, approval is automatic.
   For hardware returns, approval occurs after the returned item is received
   and inspected at our distribution center.

Q: What happens to my data if I cancel my account?
A: When you cancel your account:
   - Your data remains accessible for 30 days (grace period)
   - During grace period, you can renew without data loss
   - After 30 days, data is permanently deleted within 7 days
   - You can request a data export at any time before deletion
   - Data export formats available: CSV, JSON, Parquet
   - Enterprise customers can arrange extended archival (additional fees apply)

Q: Does ACME offer a free trial?
A: Yes! ACME offers a 14-day free trial for all subscription tiers:
   - Full access to all features of the selected tier
   - No credit card required to start
   - 1000 free AI inference credits included
   - Trial automatically converts to paid plan unless cancelled
   - One trial per organization
   - Existing customers upgrading get an extended 30-day trial on new tier

Q: How do I contact ACME customer support?
A: ACME provides multiple support channels:
   - Email: support@acme-corp.example.com (all tiers)
   - Live chat: Available via dashboard, Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM UTC
   - Phone: Enterprise tier only, 24/7 at +1-800-ACME-HELP
   - Community forum: https://community.acme-corp.example.com
   - Response times vary by tier:
     * Starter: 24 hours
     * Professional: 4 hours
     * Enterprise: 1 hour

Q: What compliance certifications does ACME have?
A: ACME Cloud Platform maintains the following certifications:
   - SOC 2 Type II (annual audit)
   - HIPAA (for healthcare data workloads)
   - GDPR (EU data protection)
   - FedRAMP Moderate (US government)
   - ISO 27001 (information security)
   - PCI DSS Level 1 (payment card processing)
   - Reports available to Enterprise customers upon NDA execution

Q: Can I use my own encryption keys with ACME Cloud?
A: Yes, ACME supports Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) encryption:
   - Available on Professional and Enterprise tiers
   - Keys managed via AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault, or Google Cloud KMS
   - Customer retains full key ownership and rotation control
   - ACME personnel cannot access BYOK-encrypted data
   - Key revocation immediately encrypts all associated data

Q: What is the maximum storage capacity per account?
A: Storage limits by tier:
   - Starter: 500GB included, expandable to 2TB ($0.10/GB/month)
   - Professional: 2TB included, expandable to 50TB ($0.08/GB/month)
   - Enterprise: Unlimited storage, custom pricing for > 50TB
   - Data transfer out: First 1TB free, then $0.09/GB
   - Data transfer in: Always free

Q: Does ACME support multi-region deployment?
A: Yes, multi-region deployment is available:
   - Enterprise tier: Unlimited regions, global load balancing
   - Professional tier: Up to 3 regions in the same geography
   - Starter tier: Single region only
   - Available regions: US East, US West, EU (Frankfurt, Ireland),
     Asia-Pacific (Tokyo, Singapore, Sydney)
   - Cross-region replication: Additional $0.02/GB/month
