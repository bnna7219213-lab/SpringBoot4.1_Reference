=== ACME CORPORATION - COMPANY OVERVIEW ===

About Us
--------
ACME Corporation is a leading cloud technology company founded in 2015,
headquartered in San Francisco, California. We provide enterprise-grade
cloud computing, data analytics, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity
solutions to over 50,000 customers worldwide across 45 countries.

Our Mission
-----------
To democratize enterprise technology by making powerful cloud, AI, and
security tools accessible to organizations of all sizes with transparent
pricing and best-in-class customer support.

2. Zero-Trust Security: Every request is authenticated, authorized, and
   encrypted. Security is built into every layer, not bolted on.

3. Open Standards: Full API compatibility with major cloud providers.
   Easy migration path with no vendor lock-in.

4. Customer-Centric SLAs: We put our money where our mouth is with
   industry-leading SLA credits and uptime guarantees.

5. Innovation Pace: Weekly feature releases, monthly major updates.
